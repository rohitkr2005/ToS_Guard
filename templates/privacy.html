Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for {{ custom_domain }}

1. Core Privacy Principle

ToS Guard is designed with strict data minimization. We do not store, archive, sell, or commercialize the legal text you submit for contract evaluation. Text submitted to the scanner is processed ephemerally in server memory solely to generate your requested risk assessment.

2. Information We Process

  • Submitted Legal Text: The text pasted into the scanner is processed in-memory during the duration of the analysis request and is discarded immediately after response generation.
  • No User Account: ToS Guard requires no user login, credit card, or personal information to access.
  • Browser Preferences: Your display mode preference (Dark, Light, or System) is stored locally in your browser storage.

3. Third Party Processors

To execute contract clause classification and score calculation, submitted text passages are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to inference endpoints for machine analysis. Processing is governed by standard encrypted transport and strict ephemeral execution.

4. Cookies and Tracking

ToS Guard does not use third party advertising cookies, commercial tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Essential browser local storage is strictly restricted to user interface settings such as theme preferences.

5. Contact and Inquiries

For questions regarding this privacy policy or data handling practices, contact support at privacy@{{ custom_domain }}.
